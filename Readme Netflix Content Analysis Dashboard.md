### **Netflix Content Analysis Dashboard**

#### 📖 **Project Overview**



This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes Netflix's content library. The dashboard provides insights into content distribution, growth trends, audience ratings, genres, countries, directors, and cast members.



The project demonstrates the complete data analytics workflow, including data cleaning, transformation, data modeling, DAX calculations, visualization, and business insight generation.



\-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### **🎯 Objectives**



* Analyze Netflix content distribution across Movies and TV Shows.
* Understand content growth trends over time.
* Identify top-performing genres and countries.
* Explore audience rating patterns.
* Analyze directors and cast contributions.
* Generate business insights through interactive dashboards.

### **📈 Key Insights**

* Netflix contains over 8,800 titles.
* Movies contribute approximately 70% of the content library.
* International Movies are the most dominant genre.
* The United States contributes the largest share of content.
* Content additions peaked during 2019.
* The majority of content targets Teen and Adult audiences.
